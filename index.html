OUR COMMITMENT

Small choices. Real impact.

We choose materials with a circular mindset, minimize unnecessary packaging and create products designed to be loved, used and responsibly let go.

Recycled Materials

Less demand for new raw materials. Our journals utilize post-consumer cotton scraps and recycled pulp.

Plastic-Conscious

Thoughtful alternatives to unnecessary plastic. From plant-wax binders to cotton twill ribbons, plastic is eliminated.

Responsible Packaging

Minimal, recyclable packaging. Every parcel arrives sealed with water-activated paper tape and soy inks.

Made to Last

Durable designs created for everyday use. Lay-flat bookbindings and resilient covers that age with grace.

OUR STORY

Good ideas deserve good materials.

Leaf & Line started with a simple question: can stationery feel premium without creating unnecessary waste? We believe the answer is yes.

We focus on tactile paper, thoughtful design and honest material choices — creating stationery that feels good in your hands and makes sense for the planet.
